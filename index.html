Why this matters

UFGS tailoring is slow.

For every project, someone manually has to:

  • 1.1Select sections from hundreds of master files
  • 1.2Remove non-applicable requirements
  • 1.3Resolve brackets and fill-ins
  • 1.4Reconcile references and submittals
  • 1.5Verify every project requirement is covered
  • 1.6Preserve SpecsIntact compatibility
  • 1.7Document why each decision was made

SpecStage takes on all seven — and keeps you in charge of every decision.

What it does

A streamlined workflow from documents to delivery.

Engineers set up a project against a pinned UFGS master library and upload their project documents into a single workspace.

SpecStage recommends the relevant sections, tailors them against the project context, and presents every decision for engineer review — in the spec itself — before anything is exported.
