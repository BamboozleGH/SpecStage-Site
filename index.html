Why this matters

UFGS tailoring is slow.

Hundreds of master sections. Dozens matter to your project, each full of brackets, fill-ins, and paragraphs that may not apply.

Today, someone has to

  • 1.1Select sections from hundreds of master files
  • 1.2Remove non-applicable requirements
  • 1.3Resolve brackets and fill-ins
  • 1.4Reconcile references and submittals
  • 1.5Cross-reference host-nation standards on OCONUS work
  • 1.6Verify every project requirement is covered
  • 1.7Preserve SpecsIntact compatibility
  • 1.8Document why each decision was made

With SpecStage

  • AI makes the first pass: brackets, fill-ins, paragraphs, table rows, references, host-nation standards
  • You review decisions, not documents: accept, override, or defer, with the rationale beside each
  • A coverage audit flags what the spec still doesn't say
  • Every choice is recorded, exportable, and approved by you

That's the difference between formatting a spec and engineering one.

The review surface

Review every decision where it lands: in the spec.

Sections render in a SpecsIntact-style view with revision marks. Each decision is anchored to the text it affects, and your call flows straight into the deliverable.

Coverage Audit

Catch what the spec never says.

SpecStage extracts the requirements in your documents, with source quotes, and checks them against the tailored spec. A spec can pass validation and still be missing something. This is the check that finds it.

  • Evidence-backed verdicts: covered, partially covered, likely gap
  • You disposition every finding; nothing closes without your call
  • AI-drafted language for confirmed gaps, placed in the spec for review
  • The full register exports to Excel

Ask the Library

Hundreds of master sections.
One question away.

Ask the installed UFGS library a question in plain language. Answers cite the exact paragraphs they came from, with the source passages quoted underneath.

  • Searches the whole library, or just the section you're reading
  • Citations are validated against the master text, never invented
  • On hand throughout tailoring, review, and the coverage audit

Your data

It stays on your desktop.

A real desktop app

SpecStage is a desktop application. Project files, UFGS masters, exports, and decision logs remain on your machine. There is no cloud workspace to upload into.

No document storage

For AI-assisted analysis, SpecStage sends only the minimum required excerpts and project context through a secured inference service to the AI model provider. SpecStage does not store project documents or tailored outputs on its servers.

Your job folder, your format

Output lands on your disk as SpecsIntact-compatible files, PDFs, and Excel, straight into the workflow you already run.
