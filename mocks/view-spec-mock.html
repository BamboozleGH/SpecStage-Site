PART 2 PRODUCTS
2.1 SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

Provide a non-structural metal roof system installed on the above deck, including panels, attachment devices, fasteners, closures, trim, flashings, and accessories, designed in accordance with ASCE 7-22 and MBMA RSDM.

2.1.1 Performance Requirements

Design the roof panel system for the wind loads indicated, with uplift resistance verified by testing in accordance with ASTM E1592 or UL 580. Submit complete sealed and signed calculations prepared by the manufacturer's registered professional engineer.

2.4 MATERIALS
2.4.2 Steel Sheet Panels

Roll-form steel sheet roof panels to the specified profile, [26] [24] 22 [20] [18] gauge and depth as indicated.

Aluminum-Zinc alloy coated [Galvanized] steel sheet conforming to ASTM A792/A792M and AISI SG03-3.

Individual panels to have continuous length sufficient to cover the entire length of any unbroken roof slope with no joints or seams, formed without warping, waviness, or ripples, and free from damage to the finish coating system.

Provide panels with thermal expansion and contraction consistent with the type of system specified, and the following profile: profile to be a 2 inch [1-1/2] high standing seam, 24 inch [18] coverage with mechanical crimping [ or][ snap-together] seams with concealed clips and fasteners.
