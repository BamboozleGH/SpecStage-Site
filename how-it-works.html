How it works

From project documents to
project-ready specifications.

SpecStage streamlines the UFGS tailoring workflow into a series of clear, engineer-controlled stages. Project documents go in; tailored, reviewable specifications come out.

1

Start from a pinned UFGS master library

Install official UFGS releases from a folder or zip and browse them in a read-only master viewer. Every project stays pinned to the library version it was created against — a new release never silently changes work in progress.

  • Folder or zip install
  • Read-only master viewer
  • Version-locked projects
2

Upload project documents

Drop your RFP, design criteria, drawings, and supporting documentation into a single project workspace. These inputs drive every downstream decision.

  • PDF
  • Word
  • Excel
  • Images
  • Text
3

AI-assisted section recommendation

SpecStage reads the documents and recommends the UFGS sections relevant to your scope. No section is processed without your confirmation.

  • Scope-based shortlist
  • Add or remove sections
  • Explicit approval
4

Project-aware tailoring

Each section is tailored against the project's full context — branch, location, scope, and the options you've configured — with the rationale recorded for every decision.

  • Brackets
  • Fill-ins
  • Paragraph applicability
  • Table rows
  • References & submittals
  • Host-nation (OCONUS)
5

Review and approve — in the spec

Sections render in a SpecsIntact-style view with revision marks, every decision anchored to the exact text it affects. Your overrides flow straight into the deliverable — and survive re-runs.

  • Accept / override / defer
  • Rationale & confidence
  • Revision marks
  • Freshness-gated approval
6

Audit requirement coverage

Requirements extracted from your documents — each with its source quote — are checked against the tailored sections. Gaps surface with evidence, and AI-drafted language lands in the spec for your review.

  • Coverage Register
  • Evidence-backed verdicts
  • Reviewer dispositions
  • AI gap drafts
  • Excel export
7

Export

Approved sections export for direct use in your existing workflow — no format changes, no new process.

  • SpecsIntact .SEC
  • Section & project PDF
  • Submittal Register (Excel + PDF)
  • Decision audit log
  • Portable .specstage bundle

Throughout: Ask the Library

At any point in the workflow, engineers can ask natural-language questions of the installed UFGS master library — "which sections govern fall protection?", "where do exposed fasteners belong?" — and get answers with citations to the exact sections they came from, scoped to the whole library or the section being read.

Engineer-controlled by design

SpecStage proposes. You decide.

Every recommendation, every tailoring decision, and every export is presented for engineer review. SpecStage is built to accelerate the engineer's work — never to replace their judgment or remove their authority over the final specification.
